# Client User Manual

## Login
- Visit the portal URL provided by your account manager.
- Use your email + password.

## Requests
- Create a new request from the Requests page.
- Add a clear title, description, and priority.
- Upload attachments (supported formats depend on portal settings).

## Documents
- Upload documents to your document library.
- Download/view documents you have access to.

## Contracts
- View contracts and download PDFs.
- Sign contracts (if enabled).

## Invoices + payments
- View invoices from the Invoices page.
- Pay online via Stripe when an invoice is marked “sent” or “overdue”.

## Notifications
- You’ll receive email notifications for key updates depending on your notification preferences.
